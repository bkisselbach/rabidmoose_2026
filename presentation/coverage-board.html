Said before you ask

where the demo could overstate the build
  • !Behavioral reranking is mechanism-proven, not results-proven. Live and trained on this org's own events — but the model is weekly, nearly empty, and most of its training data I seeded. No lift number will be claimed.
  • !Generative answers are verified on seven species, not all 1,025. Species-scoped questions answer correctly and cite their passages; type-scoped matchup questions still decline.
  • !One analytics event is missing because the protocol has no such event. The Event Protocol defines four commerce events and no impression, so slot click-through isn't computable. Left undone rather than faked.
  • !Search-result clicks aren't instrumented yet — and that one is mine, not the platform's. The recommendation rails emit ec.productClick; the /search grid doesn't, because useInteractiveProducts is wired to the rails only. No click-through number will be claimed for search.
  • !Two models are cold. Facet ranking and dynamic navigation are wired and live, but they need traffic this proof-of-concept doesn't have yet. Shown as mechanism.
